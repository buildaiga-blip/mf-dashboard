🚨 Emergency Fund (3–6 months of expenses)

Keep this in Liquid or Overnight Funds. Priority is safety and instant access, not returns — check the Liquid/Overnight table under the Debt tab.

🎯 Short-Term Goals (1–3 years)

Use Debt: Short Duration or Corporate Bond funds. Avoid equity here — a market dip right before you need the money is the main risk to manage.

🧾 Tax Saving (Section 80C)

An ELSS fund is the only equity option under 80C, with the shortest lock-in (3 years) of any 80C instrument. Suitable if you're already investing for 5+ years anyway.

📈 Long-Term Wealth Creation (5–10+ years)

Core allocation to Equity: Flexi/Multi Cap for diversification, with satellite allocations to Mid/Small Cap for higher (and more volatile) growth potential if your risk appetite allows.

🏖️ Retirement / Very Long Horizon

A mix of Equity (core) and Hybrid Balanced Advantage / Multi Asset funds, shifting gradually toward hybrid and debt as retirement approaches to reduce sequence-of-returns risk.

🛡️ Moderate Risk / Can't Stomach Big Swings

Hybrid Balanced Advantage funds auto-adjust equity/debt exposure based on market valuations — a reasonable single-fund option if you'd rather not manage the mix yourself.
